I design and build digital products that help people navigate complex problems—whether that means transforming pharmaceutical workflows into immersive VR training, creating interactive learning experiences with LEGO Games, or helping modernise Greenland's national utility infrastructure.

My background combines software development, product development and human-centred design. I enjoy working where technology, users and business needs meet, turning unclear challenges into products that feel simple and intuitive. Along the way, I've led cross-functional projects, facilitated workshops and validated ideas through 90+ user tests—contributing to measurable outcomes such as 140% improved learning outcomes and enabling trainees to acquire up to 75% of required skills before entering production.

I've always thought product development feels a bit like solving a giant puzzle. The pieces are not just technology and features—they are the people involved, the users we design for, the business goals we need to support and the real-world constraints we have to navigate. My role is often helping connect those pieces: creating shared understanding, aligning different perspectives and turning complexity into a clear direction everyone can move towards.
